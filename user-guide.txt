/statusSplitWala User Guide
====================

SplitWala is a WhatsApp bot for easy expense splitting and balance tracking within groups.

Core Commands
-------------

/split <total> [by <person> <amount> ...] [<person> owes <amount> ...] [for <description>]
    Record a new expense.
    
    Examples:
    - Equal Split (Everyone in group): /split 600 for dinner
    - Equal Split (Specific people): /split 600 @Mohit @Vipul and me
    - Complex Split (Manual contributions and debts):
      /split 4000 by me 3000 by @Vipul 1000, @Vipul owes 2500, @Mohit owes 1500 for groceries

/paid <amount> [from/by <person>] [to <person>]
    Record a payment between members.
    
    Examples:
    - You paid Mohit: /paid 200 to @Mohit
    - Mohit paid you: /paid 200 from @Mohit
    - Direct: /paid 500 from @Vipul to @Mohit
    - Natural Language: /paid 2000 to me by @Mohit
    - Clear all debt: /paid @Mohit to me (Settle everything Mohit owes you)

/balances [@person]
    Check net balances.
    
    Examples:
    - Your summary: /balances
    - Your balance with someone specific: /balances @Mohit

/summary
    Show a group scoreboard with everyone's net position.

/history [count] [@person]
    View recent transactions. Default count is 5, max is 20.
    
    Examples:
    - Recent 5: /history
    - Recent 10: /history 10
    - Recent involving Mohit: /history @Mohit 10

/delete [id | last]
    Delete a transaction and reverse its balance impact.
    
    Examples:
    - Delete most recent: /delete last
    - Delete specific: /delete abc123
    Note: Requires confirmation by typing the command followed by 'confirm'.

/resetall
    Wipe all data for the current group.
    Requires confirmation by typing '/resetall confirm'.

Tips
----
- Use "me", "i", or "myself" to refer to yourself.
- You can mention people with @ or just type their name if they are in the group.
- Descriptions can be added to /split using the 'for' keyword at the end.
- Extra spaces and capitalization are ignored.
- Amounts should be numbers up to 1,000,000.
